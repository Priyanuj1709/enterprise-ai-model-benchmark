# Labelling guidelines

Each enquiry gets ONE label: the customer's main problem.

| Label | Use when | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery | Where/when is my order, delivery options, delivery address | "Where is order #583920?" |
| Refund | Refund status, refund policy, money back for non-fault reasons | "When will my refund arrive?" |
| Payment | Payment failed, payment methods, invoices, charges | "My card was declined" |
| Product Quality | The item is faulty, broken, damaged, poor quality or not as described | "The kettle stopped working after 2 days" |
| Other | Accounts, newsletters, placing/changing/cancelling orders, general contact | "How do I delete my account?" |

## Tie-break rules
1. A faulty or damaged product is Product Quality, even if the customer asks for a refund.
   (The quality team handles fault-related refunds and replacements.)
2. Damaged on arrival is Product Quality (it is about the item's condition, not the delivery).
3. Late delivery plus a request for a refund is Delivery (the root cause is delivery).
4. If there is truly no main problem, use Other.
